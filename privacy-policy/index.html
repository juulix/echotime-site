Privacy Policy for EchoTime

Last updated: 13/11/2025

Introduction

EchoTime ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

1. Audio Data

What we collect: Voice recordings when you use the voice recognition feature

How we use it: To convert your speech to text and process your shopping list requests, reminders, and calendar events

Storage: Audio data is temporarily processed and not permanently stored on our servers

2. Location Data

What we collect: Your device's location when you enable geofence notifications

How we use it: To send notifications when you're near stores you've added to your shopping lists

Storage: Location data is stored locally on your device and not transmitted to our servers

3. User Identifiers

What we collect: User ID and Device ID for app functionality and analytics

How we use it: To track usage quotas, provide app functionality, and analyze app performance

Storage: Identifiers are stored on our servers for quota management and analytics purposes

4. Shopping List Data

What we collect: Product names, categories, and shopping list information

How we use it: To provide AI-powered product categorization and suggestions

Storage: Data is stored locally on your device and may be processed by our AI service

5. Calendar and Reminder Data

What we collect: Event titles, dates, and reminder information

How we use it: To create and manage your calendar events and reminders

Storage: Data is stored locally on your device using Apple's native frameworks

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

Provide voice recognition and text conversion services

Categorize products using AI technology

Send location-based notifications

Manage your shopping lists and calendar events

Track usage quotas and provide app functionality

Improve our app's functionality and user experience

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except:

When required by law

To protect our rights and safety

With your explicit consent

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

Access your personal information

Correct inaccurate information

Delete your data

Withdraw consent for data processing

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and on our website.
